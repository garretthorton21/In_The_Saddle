# Asset wishlist (steal with pride)

Claim free stuff early — Epic's monthly Fab freebies are keep-forever, and
you want the library built before you need it.

## Free, in-engine

- **Quixel Bridge + Megascans** — ships with UE5. Photoreal rocks, trees,
  grass, ground materials. This is the entire valley, free.
- **Engine sample content** — the Third Person template's mannequin and
  starter props are fine as placeholders.

## Free, claim monthly

- **Fab limited-time free** — https://www.fab.com/limited-time-free
  New packs every month, yours forever once claimed. Check monthly; environment
  and animation packs show up regularly.

## Reference (read, don't build on)

- **Lyra Starter Game** — free sample project in the Epic Launcher. Epic's
  current "how we think games should be architected" reference. Useful when
  the prototype outgrows template Blueprints.

## The horse (the crux asset — decide at M2)

No good free horse-with-rider exists; this is the one thing likely worth
paying for. Candidates on Fab:

- **The Viking Horse: Icelandic Horse with Rider Animations** —
  https://www.fab.com/listings/65a2e113-9f11-4c89-bdca-088b78c9176d
  Rigged horse, modular tack, 10 coats, 34 locomotion anims + 49 rider anims.
- **Horse Champion Jumper with rider animations** —
  https://www.fab.com/listings/aea7f926-e6b0-4771-9149-42213556f6be
  Sport horse, 8 coats, full rider set.
- Both pair with a "Complete Horse Riding System" asset for mount/dismount
  and blended locomotion — budget for the pair.

Free alternative: **Horse Kit** project template on itch.io (UE 4.2–5.6) —
full riding system with Animation Blueprints, mount/dismount, terrain
alignment. Older, but free and complete.

## Rule of thumb

Environment = free (Megascans). Systems = built (ours). Horse = the one
check we write. Everything else waits until M1 exists.
